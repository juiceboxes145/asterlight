## Project summary

Asterlight is an interactive web application that makes NASA's SPHEREx infrared sky observations easier to explore. Users can enter celestial coordinates, retrieve available images, and play them as a time sequence to investigate how a region of the sky changes.

Built for the Planet X and SPHEREx challenge, Asterlight aligns observations to a shared sky view and provides playback, reference blinking, quality masks, and image subtraction for compatible wavelength bands. Users can also record candidate changes, download observation reports, and share coordinates for further investigation.

Infrared observations can reveal objects and activity that are difficult to study in visible light, but interpreting astronomical data often requires specialized tools. Asterlight lowers that barrier through an accessible browser interface, helping students, educators, and citizen scientists explore real observations. Apparent changes require careful checks for instrument effects, wavelength differences, and other sources of uncertainty.
